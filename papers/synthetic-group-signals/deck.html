Synthetic data curation at group granularity

  • Effective Synthetic Data Curation Requires Group-Level Signals
  • Cathy Jiao and Chenyan Xiong, arXiv v1, September 30, 2026
  • Technical reading for AI data infrastructure engineering
核心问题：单条样本分数的求和能否代表整组训练价值？本文用受控实验说明二者可能偏离。
Paper v1

The unit of selection changes the objective

  • Individual influence estimates a sample’s utility in isolation.
  • A group changes the model through multiple interacting optimization steps.
  • Synthetic paraphrases share origins and may concentrate training signal despite high individual scores.
作者研究训练价值的非加性。不要把 group 简化成按来源分桶后的平均质量分。
Paper v1

Proxy oracle and group oracle

  • The proxy aggregates individual sample effects around the reference model.
  • The group oracle evaluates the validation-loss change after training on the group.
  • Matching proxy scores while varying composition isolates interactions that the additive proxy misses.
Oracle 是昂贵的实验参照，不是可直接用于全量生产的免费标签。参见第 2 节与附录 B.1。
Paper v1

Controlled group construction

  • BGE embeddings and k-means partition each corpus into 20 semantic clusters.
  • Groups span one to 20 clusters while their average individual proxy scores remain matched.
  • Gumbel-Top-k sampling selects groups under matched training budgets.
既控制平均单样本效用，又改变语义与主题组成，避免仅用更高质量数据解释结果。第 3.2 节。

Synthetic recycling increases redundancy pressure

  • The pool contains 716,800 sequences for every rephrase factor.
  • Rephrase factors 1, 10, and 50 reduce unique source documents as repetitions rise.
  • FineWeb-Edu, BM25, and GradSim represent individual scoring. GMRel and GREATS include relations.
r=50 是高合成度压力测试。固定序列总数使来源覆盖度的变化更容易解释。第 4 节。

Gradient diversity targets expensive audits

  • Within-group gradient diversity predicts the proxy–oracle gap.
  • Reported Spearman correlations range from 0.58 to 0.63 across tested settings.
  • Audit high-diversity groups with the group oracle and use a cheaper proxy elsewhere.
这是预算分配诊断，不是梯度越多样训练价值就一定越高。高 gap 意味着需要更准确估计。第 5 节。
Paper v1

Limits of the evidence

  • The oracle depends on the chosen validation objective and training horizon.
  • Groups are deliberately constructed. Real production mixtures may have different interactions.
  • Gradient extraction adds compute and memory overhead even when subsampling helps.
作者结果支持关系信号值得保留，但不能推出任何 diversity 策略都能提升任意训练任务。
Paper v1

Curation data model implications

  • Inference: preserve synthetic parent IDs and generator versions alongside each record.
  • Inference: materialize group membership and selection runs rather than only a scalar quality column.
  • Proposed application: store group diagnostics and downstream evaluation deltas as versioned artifacts.
工程建议由机制推导，并非论文交付的产品功能。需要可追踪 group 才能复查选数决策。
Paper v1

A bounded reproduction plan

  • Rebuild a small proxy-matched pool and compare random, GradSim, and one group method.
  • Fix selected tokens, training steps, model initialization, and held-out tasks across arms.
  • Measure generation metrics, oracle rank recovery, gradient memory, and audit cost separately.
先验证选择信号是否可迁移，再投资全量梯度计算。本文阅读未执行训练，也未验证专用公开代码仓库。
Paper v1

Adoption decision

  • Published claim: group interactions explain selection failures in the tested synthetic settings.
  • Inference: per-record scores alone provide an incomplete selection contract.
  • Proposed gate: adopt a group diagnostic only after gains survive matched token and compute budgets.
决策标准是训练效用与成本共同改善，不能只看离线分数相关性。来源入口及逐页讲解见 sources.md。
